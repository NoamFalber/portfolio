About

I like building the parts other people build with.

I’m strongest with C#, Unity, and MonoGame. I build gameplay mechanics and reusable systems, and I spend a lot of time on the custom tools around them: interfaces that let designers and artists build spells, lay out entire maps, and iterate inside the project.

I’ve worked both solo and with dedicated teams, and I prefer the back-and-forth of team development. The work I gravitate toward sits where code, design, and art overlap: shaders, post-processing, GPU-intensive effects, volumetric clouds, and the systems that make them easier to build.
