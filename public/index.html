About

Two disciplines,
one practice.

I’m a Developer and Technical Artist. My work sits between the systems that make a game function and the visual decisions that shape how it feels.

This portfolio keeps those two sides together: the visible result first, then the work and decisions behind it. The goal is to make each project quick to understand and useful to inspect.
